## What it does

22 behavioral questions. One structural pattern. Depending on tier — zero, one, or three exits.

The engine scores your answers across three axes — Drive, Reality, Capacity — and outputs one of 12 decision patterns. Each pattern reveals:

- What is breaking
- What it is costing you
- The next structural move (if your tier includes exits)

## How it works

The engine runs entirely deterministically — no AI generation in the report itself. Your answers are scored, a behavioral pattern is identified, and a fixed diagnostic is returned. The output is the same every time for the same pattern.

The black box is intentional. The axis names, scoring logic, and archetype mapping are not exposed.
